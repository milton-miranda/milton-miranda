## 👨‍💻 About me

I work at the intersection of **software engineering, artificial intelligence and platform operations**. My experience spans full-stack applications, machine learning, LLM engineering, DevOps, MLOps and observability, with applications in healthcare, personal care, finance, education, research and industry.

What connects my work is an interest in the complete system: **the application people use, the intelligence behind it, and the infrastructure that keeps it running.**

- 🧠 **AI & ML:** applications involving LLM-assisted decision support, medical imaging and smartwatch-based health analysis.
- 🛠️ **Software:** full-stack development with Python, C/C++, Go and Java, alongside Node.js, Next.js and frontend technologies.
- ⚙️ **Operations:** DevOps, MLOps and observability as part of the engineering lifecycle.
- 🚀 **Products:** two data and AI-enabled SaaS ecosystems — **Vectrion** and **DataPlataforms**.
- 🔬 **Domains:** neurocognition, systematic reviews, education, financial workflows and industrial reliability.
- 🌍 **Career interests:** international opportunities in AI/ML, full-stack and platform engineering.
